Assumptions & calibration

Estimated damage for successful, noncritical hits. No spell failures, misses, shields, level penalties, damage-over-time ticks or monster healing are simulated. Physical skill and gear formulas are approximate. Plans compare at most 12 selected skills and your cast/melee limits; “Fewest casts” does not mean fastest real kill time.

Add an HP margin for regeneration and hidden decimals. Calibration scales all hit damage; 100% uses the research formula.

Magic: 91 × skill power × √(M. Atk. × shot factor) ÷ M. Def., then elemental modifiers. Shot factors: 1 / 2 / 4 for none / regular / blessed. Melee: 70 × P. Atk. ÷ P. Def.; Soulshots double the attack term. Physical skills use P. Atk. plus skill power, with no Soulshot bonus. Damage is rounded down per hit.
